Role Construction Subteam & Timed Builder

Overview

As a core member of the construction subteam for UW's AISC Steel Bridge Team, I participate in the end-to-end fabrication and timed assembly of a scale steel bridge for regional and national intercollegiate competitions.

Fabrication & Construction

Day to day, my work involves welding and machining structural members, precise shop fitting, and practicing rapid field assembly. As a member of the official build team during competition, I directly executed the timed assembly of the bridge on the floor under strict competition rules and tolerances.

Competition Results

At the regional competition hosted by the University of Alaska Anchorage, our team earned 2nd Place Overall, securing our spot at the national finals.

  • 2nd Place: Construction Speed
  • 2nd Place: Lightness
  • 2nd Place: Stiffness
  • 2nd Place: Efficiency
  • 2nd Place: Economy
  • 4th Place: Aesthetics

Advancing to the AISC Student Steel Bridge Competition National Finals at UTEP (University of Texas at El Paso), our team placed 18th nationally out of top-qualifying universities across the country.
