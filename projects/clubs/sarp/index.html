Overview

As part of the Society for Advanced Rocket Propulsion (SARP) at the University of Washington, my work focuses on practical component fabrication and composite manufacturing research for high-power rocket systems.

Aluminum Machining & Drawings

I use engineering drawings to machine custom aluminum parts on a manual mill. My hands-on work involves taking raw stock, setting up the mill, and cutting the material down to size to match the required tolerances for the rocket.

Composite Coupler Research

Rocket couplers join two airframe sections, so they need to be stiff and light. I'm looking into making fiberglass couplers by laying up cloth over a 3D-printed mold instead of machining or buying them.

The mold is a two-piece design. I first modeled it in Blender and am now rebuilding it in Fusion so I can change it for layup. The main fix so far is rounding off sharp corners, because they rip the vacuum bag when you bag the mold.

The variables I'm testing are mold geometry, surface prep on the printed plastic, release agents, and layup technique. The goal is a coupler that comes off the mold clean and doesn't need much cleanup afterward.
