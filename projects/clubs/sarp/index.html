Overview

I work with the Society for Advanced Rocket Propulsion at the University of Washington on propulsion and structural hardware for student-built rocket systems.

Fabrication

My work includes interpreting technical engineering drawings, preparing raw material, and operating shop equipment to manufacture structural and propulsion components.

Design to Hardware

The project connects CAD and manufacturing. Components begin as engineering designs and drawings before being turned into physical hardware through machining and shop fabrication.
