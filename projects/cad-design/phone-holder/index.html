Design / CAD

Phone Holder

A mechanical phone holder designed as a compact, functional assembly. The design was developed in SolidWorks and modeled as a complete assembly with moving components.

Category Mechanical Design
Tools SolidWorks · 3D Printing
Status Completed ✔

Overview

This project focused on designing a phone holder as a complete mechanical assembly. The model was developed in CAD with the individual components designed to work together as a single mechanism.

Design

The design process involved creating the individual components, assembling them together, and checking that the mechanism moved as intended. The final assembly is shown above in both an animated view and an interactive 3D model.
