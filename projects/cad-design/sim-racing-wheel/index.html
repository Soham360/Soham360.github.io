Design / CAD

Racing Sim Wheel

A 3D-printed sim racing steering wheel built around a magnetic rotary encoder and a microcontroller that reports steering position over USB. It touches mechanical design, electronics, CAD, fabrication, and embedded programming.

Category Mechanical / Electronics
Tools SolidWorks · Arduino · 3D Printing
Status In Progress

Overview

I wanted a wheel that actually works with PC racing games, not just a display piece, so the whole build is centered on getting real steering data to the computer over USB.

Mechanical Design

A steel shaft runs through bearings at the center, with everything around it 3D printed. The tricky part was keeping the mechanism compact while still giving the wheel roughly 540 degrees of rotation.

Electronics

A magnetic rotary encoder tracks the shaft's position, and a microcontroller reads that value and sends it to the computer over USB.
