Design / CAD

Racing Sim Wheel

A custom 3D-printed sim racing steering wheel designed around a USB-connected microcontroller and magnetic position sensing. The project combines mechanical design, electronics, CAD, fabrication, and embedded programming.

Category Mechanical / Electronics
Tools Fusion 360 · Arduino · 3D Printing
Status In Progress

Overview

The goal of this project is to build a functional sim racing steering wheel from the ground up. The wheel is designed to connect to a Windows computer over USB and report steering position to the computer.

Mechanical Design

The wheel uses a steel shaft supported by bearings, with the surrounding components designed to be 3D printed. The design focuses on keeping the mechanism compact while allowing the wheel to rotate through approximately 540 degrees.

Electronics

Steering position is measured using a magnetic rotary encoder connected to a microcontroller. The controller reads the steering position and communicates it to the computer through USB.
